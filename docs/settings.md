Open Settings with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>,</kbd> or **View → Settings**. Changes
apply at once and are saved in `~/.config/2fip/settings.json`.

With the keyboard: <kbd>Tab</kbd> or <kbd>↓</kbd> / <kbd>↑</kbd> move between
settings, <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>Enter</kbd> ticks a box, <kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>→</kbd> change a
list's choice, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the panel.

## General

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Show hidden files** | Also lists files and folders whose name starts with a dot. Off by default; <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>H</kbd> switches it too. |
| **Show the extension in its own column** | List view: separate Name and Ext columns, like Total Commander. |
| **Show thumbnails** | Previews of images, PDFs, videos and fonts, shared with other file managers through `~/.cache/thumbnails`. |
| **Language** | System default, Deutsch, English, Español (España), Español (Latinoamérica), Français, Italiano, Português (Portugal) or Português (Brasil). |

## Theme

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Color theme** | **System** follows COSMIC's appearance; or choose Light, Dark, Dracula, Everforest, Gruvbox Material, Nord, Tokyo Night Storm, Catppuccin Mocha, Catppuccin Macchiato, Ayu Dark, Matrix, Monokai Pro, Solarized Dark or Gruvbox Dark. It affects 2fip only. |
| **Icon style** | **Colorful** (like COSMIC Files) or **Monochrome**. |
| **File name size** | Default (14 px), Small (13 px), Smaller (12 px) or Tiny (11 px): smaller text shows more files. |
